Website Overview
MDRN Skin is a premium men's skincare brand based in Leeds, United Kingdom. The business offers performance-focused skincare products designed to hydrate, protect, and support skin recovery from everyday environmental exposure.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting MDRN as a modern, premium skincare brand, highlighting its HyPrEn™ Skin Performance System and helping men discover products suited to their daily skincare needs.
Main Improvements
1. Clearer Brand Positioning
The homepage gives greater prominence to MDRN's core HyPrEn™ concept: Hydrate, Protect and Enrich. This creates a clearer brand message and communicates the purpose of its performance skincare range.

2. Improved Product Presentation
The product range and HyPrEn™ Skin Performance System are given clearer presentation, making it easier for visitors to understand the available skincare solutions and explore individual products.

3. Stronger Shopping Experience
The homepage places greater emphasis on product discovery and the direct-to-consumer shopping journey, helping visitors move from understanding the brand to exploring its skincare products.
Design Improvements
- Visual Presentation: A more refined, premium visual direction supports MDRN's modern men's skincare positioning.
- Layout: Improved content hierarchy gives greater visibility to the brand concept, skincare system, and product range.
- User Experience: A clearer browsing structure helps visitors understand the product offering and navigate the shopping experience.
- Brand Consistency: The design reinforces the HyPrEn™ concept and maintains a cohesive presentation across key homepage sections.
- Overall Appearance: The homepage adopts a cleaner, more contemporary appearance aligned with the brand's performance-focused skincare identity.
Project Objective
The redesign aims to strengthen MDRN Skin's online brand presence, communicate its skincare philosophy more effectively, and create a clearer product discovery experience for men looking for straightforward, premium skincare solutions.
