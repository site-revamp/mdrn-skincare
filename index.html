HyPrEn™ is MDRN's five-product skincare system built around three principles: Hydrate, Protect and Enrich. It combines daily skincare essentials with weekly treatments.

MDRN positions its products for men whose skin experiences everyday exposure from training, UV, pollution, screens and modern lifestyles.

The complete system contains five products: Blue Recovery Serum, Hydration Layer Mask, Volcanic Clay, Hydrogel Eye Patches and Hydrating Crème SPF15.
